The circle

A team that behaves like family

Vikrama is not a club with a membership card. It is a group of people who found each other on the court and decided the rest of life was better together too.

We rally until the lights go off. We travel when someone has a weekend free and a destination in mind. We show up for weddings, birthdays, housewarmings, and the days that do not come with an invitation — hospital visits, moving boxes, a late-night call.

When one of us is in trouble, the rest of us move. When someone outside the circle needs help, we move then too. Friendship is the starting point. Loyalty is the practice.

On court

Badminton is how we found each other

Nights that turn into years

We play for the rally, not the ranking. Singles, doubles, mixed chaos, and the kind of banter that only happens when you already trust the person on the other side of the net.

  • Weekly court sessions — sweat, jokes, extra games
  • Friendly matches with anyone who wants a racket
  • Beginners welcome. Ego stays in the locker.

Off court

Travel, gather, and take care

01

Travel

Hill stations, city weekends, last-minute trains. We pack light and bring the same energy we bring to a match — curious, loud, and looking after the slowest walker.

02

Functions & parties

If there is a celebration, Vikrama is in the room. We dance, we eat too much, we stay to stack chairs. Joy is a team sport.

03

When someone is in trouble

We do not wait to be asked twice. Rides, money, company at 2 a.m., a listening ear. That is the deal we made without writing it down.

04

Help beyond the circle

The same muscle we use for each other, we use for others — neighbors, friends of friends, anyone who needs a crew that actually shows up.
